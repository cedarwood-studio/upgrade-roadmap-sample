## Sources of the rules

The rules listed in `roadmap/measurements/matches.json` summarize changes documented by the Rails and Ruby projects in their guides, release notes and component changelogs. Each rule links its source. The summaries are ours. End-of-life dates come from [endoflife.date](https://endoflife.date), and gem metadata from [rubygems.org](https://rubygems.org).
